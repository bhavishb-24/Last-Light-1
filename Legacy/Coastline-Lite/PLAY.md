# Last Light Lite

A compact standalone TI-84 Evo driving game.

## Install

Extract Coastline-Evo-Lite.zip. Send LASTLITE.py to the calculator with TI Connect Evo. Run it from PRGM → Python App → LASTLITE.

## Play

Press ENTER for the delivery shift or 2nd for free drive. Choose an adjacent district with the arrows. During a drive, LEFT and RIGHT change lanes, UP accelerates and DOWN brakes. Press 2nd to check the map. CLEAR exits.

The shift has four deliveries across a connected 12-district city. This compact edition focuses on driving and the map. It does not include Coastline's walking, phone, interiors, or expanded side activities.
