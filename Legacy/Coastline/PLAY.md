# Last Light · Coastline

A third-person open-world game for the TI-84 Evo. Play as Lena and Sol on the night someone sells the emergency radio frequency. Find the missing witness, follow the relief money, and decide whose voice goes on air. You can leave the story at a street corner to fish, run a job, work out, or take a floatplane across the bay. The city stays open after the credits.

## Get it onto your calculator

1. Unzip the download on your computer. Keep the original names of all ten files ending in `.py`.
2. Turn on the Evo, plug it in with a USB data cable, and open TI Connect Evo in desktop Chrome. Choose **Connect to Calculator**, select your device, then choose **Send Files → Send to Calculator**. [TI Connect Evo](https://connectevo.ti.com/)
3. Select all ten Python files. If the browser offers a destination, choose **RAM**. Confirm **Send** and wait for the success message. The guide stays on your computer.
4. Press **PRGM → Python App**, highlight **COASTRUN**, and choose **Run**. Press **ENTER** at the sunset title screen to begin. The other nine programs supply the geography, renderer and game systems. [TI transfer guide](https://education.ti.com/en/product-resources/eguides/eguide-84-evo/evo-connect-user-guide)

If Python reports a missing COAST module, reopen the Python file list and check all ten names against the extracted folder. If it reports a memory error, exit and reopen the Python app for a fresh session. The game runs offline after transfer.

If you are upgrading from the previous Coastline release, copy your checkpoint before leaving the game. Transfer the full set together and replace the existing COAST files when prompted. Previous Coastline checkpoint codes load in this edition.

## Three drives worth taking

- **For the skyline**, leave WAVE in West Quay and head east toward Glasshouse. Turn south at the downtown crossroads toward Lantern Square. Press **TRACE** to lift the camera. The glass offices reflect the sky, condo balconies wrap their corners and the windows light up after dark. For the skyline across the bay, take a skiff from Marina and look southwest from the north shore.
- **For the beach**, pin Marina in the atlas. The shore road curves out over the sandy headland between FM Tower and Marina. Continue past the cabanas to the palm promenade and pier shelter. The pastel hotels have stepped rooflines, horizontal window bands and portholes inspired by Miami Beach architecture. [Miami Design Preservation League](https://mdpl.org/miami-design-styles/what-is-art-deco/)
- **For the backwaters**, follow the east road through the freight cranes at East Docks. Turn south into Tin Marsh and pick up the dirt track on the far side of the crossroads. It ends on a mangrove headland. The two main channels open to the south. You can steer a skiff under the road crossings or drive across them on the surface.

The region has 60 solid building footprints, including 10 high-rises, across 16 districts. The atlas shows the same buildings and coastline you travel through. Small anchor symbols mark the four coastal launches. **2nd** opens the atlas, **DEL** toggles district zoom, the arrows move between districts and **ENTER** pins the district. The four offshore keys are landmarks to circle by water or overfly. Landing on foot is available at the four ports. The new towers are exterior landmarks. To visit a room, use the directory at a marked venue door.

## Your first five minutes

The WAVE studio stands straight ahead at West Quay. Tap **UP** to start walking. **DOWN** stops you. When the bottom line names the studio, press **ENTER** to meet Mara. Open the atlas with **2nd** to see your next destination in gold. The pink square is your parked compact. Walk beside it and press **ENTER** to drive.

You can also open the phone with **DEL**, select **Call taxi**, and ride to Sun Pier for $6. The taxi leaves you in the intersection. The shop entrance sits just northeast of the crossing. Follow the gold marker and press **ENTER** when the venue name appears.

## Controls

| Where | Input | Action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Street or coast | UP / DOWN | Start or accelerate / brake. On the street another DOWN from a stop reverses. Movement continues after a tap. |
| Street or coast | LEFT / RIGHT | Turn. Tap repeatedly for a sharper turn on OS versions that report one press per hold. |
| Street | ENTER | Talk, interact, collect, drive or park. On an ordinary venue door this opens the story or its main service. |
| Street | 2nd | Open the city atlas. DEL zooms, arrows select a district, ENTER pins it, CLEAR returns. |
| Street | TRACE | Switch between the street and raised skyline camera. Works on foot or in a vehicle. |
| Street | DEL | Raise the phone. UP and DOWN select apps, ENTER opens, CLEAR returns. |
| Street | MODE | Open actions, crew, loadout, local services, room directory or coastal transport. |
| Street | ALPHA or CLEAR | Open the journal. ENTER resumes. CLEAR from the journal exits the game. |
| Journal | DEL / 2nd | Show your checkpoint / toggle Full and Fast graphics. |
| Coast | ENTER / CLEAR | Go ashore near a landing / call a tow back to the starting port. While submerged, ENTER searches the wreck. |
| Coast | 2nd | View navigation information. In a floatplane, cycle three altitudes. While swimming, dive or resurface. |

The red meter shows health, mint shows Focus, and gold shows fuel when driving. The corner radar is north-up. The compass shows your heading. Stars are local to the current character and region. Fast graphics shortens the view distance and reduces scenery. The game starts in Full and can lower detail automatically if drawing three consecutive street frames takes too long.

## Save and continue

Open the journal and press **DEL**, or use the phone’s **Checkpoint** app. Photograph the four rows or copy them in order. At the title screen press **2nd**. Type one row per prompt and press **ENTER** after each row. The code has 74 characters, in groups of 20, 20, 20 and 14. Uppercase and lowercase letters both work. A checksum checks what you typed.

A checkpoint restores the district, story, earnings, savings, both characters, local wanted stars, vehicle collection and permanent finds. You resume on foot with a parked car. Timed activities restart at their entry points, and daylight restarts. The game keeps its state in the Python session and your checkpoint code. Quitting without copying the current code discards that session’s new progress.

At any venue door, choose **MODE → Work / interiors**. The menu offers street activities, supplies, residents, rooms, gym, animals, garage and neighborhood jobs. A district without its own activity offers a directory that can pin another venue. The room directory has four floors with four doors each. Use the arrows to walk up to the counter before pressing ENTER. West Quay and Beacon Hill each have a safehouse on floor 1 behind door 1. Hill’s lease costs $90. At home you can recover, change your jacket, trim your hair, call your partner, store a nearby ride and read a checkpoint.

## Systems you can use together

- **Crew and reputation.** After the tower repair, choose MODE → Request partner to switch, meet up or go solo. The partners have independent health, equipment, training and criminal profiles. Exploring and completing activities together builds four levels of relationship. The tape you choose determines the ending. Savings, bond and your active lead’s reputation affect the epilogue.
- **Money and heat.** Carry proceeds in your wallet or deposit them in the phone bank. The clinic charges up to $25 on defeat. An arrest costs up to $35. Savings survive both. A witness can alert patrols. At three stars roadblocks appear, and at four stars a helicopter becomes visible. Search intensity depends on the lead’s local stars and profile. Surrender is available through the crew menu below the two highest profile tiers.
- **Street vehicles.** Eight classes span compact, muscle, sports, luxury, supercar, pickup, ATV and motorbike. The scanner pins the closest lot when out of range. Stand by the parked vehicle to see its value, security tier, alarm, tracker and registration fee. The arcade lock uses keypad cues and fictional access tokens. Register a car, or store it at a lockup, to summon it through Delivery. Standard delivery costs $4. A $60 premium pass covers subsequent deliveries. Wet roads reduce grip, rain tires help, and a damaged vehicle loses speed.
- **Coastal travel.** At the Marina, Hill, Marsh or Breakwater venue door, choose MODE → Coastal transport. Pick a skiff, floatplane, jet ski, airboat or swim, then pin a landing. You steer continuously and can land at any of the four ports. Boats follow the water around the mainland. The plane can cross over it. The sunken cutter lies southeast of the mainland and south of the Breakwater causeway. Dive and use ENTER near the hull to recover a museum bell.
- **Phone and social.** Transfer money, call a taxi, get a car, check the forecast and police radio, post a selfie, or find a nearby live event. The camera offers a selfie toggle and three aperture-frame settings. Its local feed remembers your latest photo location and post count. Pin a live event, visit its venue while active, and join it. Scan a storefront mural in each of the six regions.
- **Fitness and appearance.** A $15 gym membership unlocks six keypad workouts. Strength changes maximum health, cardio changes stamina, and skill improves Focus duration. Food, rest, workouts and time affect hunger, energy and appearance. Clothing, tattoos, barbers, supplies and pet snacks are sold in town. Offering a dog a snack brings it to your safehouse.
- **Street encounters.** MODE → Arcade aim opens an encounter against a moving target. LEFT/RIGHT aim, UP locks on, DOWN takes cover, and ENTER spends ammunition. Press 2nd to reload, spend Focus, de-escalate or attempt a nonlethal takedown from cover or a crouch. Buy ammunition at a supplies counter. Three street caches hold reserve ammunition, a revolver and first aid.
- **Work and discoveries.** Follow the neighborhood job from Kiln Yard to East Docks to Glasshouse. Bring your partner for the final mosaic puzzle. Other options include deliveries, six collectible records, murals, a shipwreck, three races, fishing with lure-dependent weights, sports, music, water and flight challenges.

## How this edition plays

The exterior uses a software perspective renderer for the roads and buildings and scaled artwork for people and vehicles. The phone, rooms and short challenges use 2D graphics. The 256 room addresses reuse furnished layouts. Water and flight use arcade handling. The other crew member can travel beside you. The encounter is a separate aim mode, and radio stations and social posts are delivered as text. Audio music, recorded video, a full passenger AI, ragdolls, per-wheel suspension and a full aircraft physics simulator are outside this edition.

The ten files are plain Python. During play the game uses TI drawing, key input and the clock, with its own data in memory. It never calls a file writer, OS installer, reset command or calculator-variable storage API.

## What was checked

A desktop harness drove the entire story through the street map using movement and interaction inputs. Tests also covered both endings, all three race medals, all 256 room addresses, 300 randomized checkpoint round trips, 640 rendered street views, all five coastal travel modes, port-to-port skiff and floatplane trips, and the bank, crew, shops and side activities. Map checks covered four barrier keys, both shore drives, clearance at the bridges and all 16 venue doors. All ten files compiled with upstream MicroPython 1.13. On a 190 KiB test heap, a host build of that interpreter ran both endings, cycled through phone, gym and coastal scenes, and restored checkpoints using simulated TI input and drawing. Physical transfer, TI firmware execution and handheld frame rate still need a real Evo.

Texas Instruments lists a 156 MHz Evo processor, 3.5 MB of user-available memory and a 320 by 240 pixel display. The Evo supports both TI-Basic and Python programming. Coastline uses Python and the 320 by 210 pixel drawing area. The story, city, cast, artwork and dialogue are original. Rockstar’s linked protagonists and regional storytelling in VI and switchable mission roles in V informed this design.
